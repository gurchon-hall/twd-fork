About the deck in general:
Try to get a vampire out on turn one, preferably with superior oblivion. Your first action should always be to recruit a Spectral Servitor and start bleeding – the deck can’t afford to fall behind on bleeds.
9 times out of 10 you want to pay the upkeep for your Servitors and keep bleeding with them to maintain pressure. Try to avoid going down to 0 blood on your vampires, keep bringing out new vampires top to keep playing Servitors. Vampires on 1 blood can bleed like a servitor.
Notes from the finals:
Kalle Blomgren (!Nosferatu Cathari bleed) -> Tim Karlsson (Hecata swarm bleed) -> Magnus Söder (Malkavian stealth bleed) -> Marcus Berg (Lasombra princes bleed & vote, top seed) -> Helena Löf (!Malkavian kindred spirits bleed)
I drew Dreams of the Sphinx in my starting hand and could bring out Alek König on turn one. My early pressure forced my prey to bring out lower cap vampires in order to not fall behind. All decks went aggressively forward and Kalle was ousted on turn 5, then Magnus and Helena were ousted on turn 6. I had paid upkeep for every Servitor during the game and Marcus could not handle my swarm of minions in the heads up.
